Historical Context

Origin
Spain's presence can be traced back to Santa Cruz de la Mar Pequeña in 1476. Interest rekindled in the 19th century leading to the cession of the territory by Morocco in 1860.
Establishment
1934, after Colonel Osvaldo Capaz took possession on behalf of the Second Spanish Republic.
Ifni War
The Moroccan Army of Liberation attacked the territory in 1957, leading to the War of Ifni.
Retrocession To Morocco
On June 30, 1969, Spain ceded Ifni to Morocco by the Treaty of Fez, ratified on April 22, 1969.

Modern Territory

Part of Morocco, known as Sidi Ifni, encompassing a larger territory than the former Spanish province.
