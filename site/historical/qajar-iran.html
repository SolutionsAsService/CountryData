Summary

Qajar Iran, ruled by the Qajar dynasty of Turkic origin, was a significant period in Iranian history, spanning from 1789 to 1925. It began with Agha Mohammad Khan Qajar's consolidation of power and included notable events like several treaties and the Persian Constitutional Revolution. The era saw a transition from absolute to constitutional monarchy and was marked by its cultural, linguistic, and governmental developments. It ended with the rise of Reza Pahlavi and the establishment of Pahlavi Iran.
