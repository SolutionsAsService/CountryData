Notes

Transcaucasian SFSRIntegration
Azerbaijan, along with the Armenian SSR and Georgian SSR, was incorporated into the Transcaucasian SFSR, becoming part of the Soviet Union in December 1922.
Constitution Changes
The Constitution of Azerbaijan SSR, approved on 14 March 1937, was in effect until the adoption of the new Constitution of Azerbaijan in 1995.
