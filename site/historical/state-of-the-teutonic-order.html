Overview

Establishment
Formed by the knights of the Teutonic Order during the early 13th century Northern Crusades in Prussia. Expanded through the Prussian Crusade against Baltic Prussians and the invasions of Poland and Lithuania.
Merger
1237, Livonian Brothers of the Sword merged with the Teutonic Order, becoming its Livonian Order branch.
Colonization
German and Polish colonization followed conquests.
Territory Acquisitions
Acquisition of Duchy of Estonia in 1346 from the King of Denmark.

Historical Context

Old Prussians
Resisted many conquest attempts before Teutonic Knights.
Conrad Of Masovia
Intensified conquest attempts leading to Crusades in the early 13th century.
