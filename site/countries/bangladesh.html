History

Ancient Bengal
Known as Gangaridai, a bastion of pre-Islamic kingdoms.
Muslim Conquests
After 1204, led to the sultanate and Mughal periods.
Bengal Sultanate
An independent Bengal Sultanate was established.
Mughal Bengal
Wealthy Mughal Bengal, important in regional affairs, trade, and diplomacy.
British Era
Bengal Presidency
Bengal's administrative jurisdiction reached its greatest extent.
Eastern Bengal and Assam
Creation in 1905 set a precedent for Bangladesh's emergence.
Partition of Bengal
Bengal was divided during the Partition of India in 1947.
Dominion of Pakistan
East Bengal
Became East Pakistan in 1947.
Dhaka Legislative Capital
Dhaka became the legislative capital.
Liberation War
In 1971, led to the independence of Bangladesh.
Bangladesh Genocide
Massacre of pro-independence Bengali civilians.
