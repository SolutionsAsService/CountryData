History

Turkmenistan has a rich history and was once an important stop on the Silk Road. It was annexed by the Russian Empire in 1881 and later became a constituent republic of the Soviet Union. It gained independence in 1991.

Human Rights

The country is widely criticized for its poor human rights record, treatment of minorities, and lack of press and religious freedoms.

Leadership

Turkmenistan has been ruled by repressive regimes, including President for Life Saparmurat Niyazov, Gurbanguly Berdimuhamedow, and his son Serdar.

Natural Resources

Turkmenistan possesses the world's fifth-largest reserves of natural gas.

Geography

Most of the country is covered by the Karakum Desert.

International Relations

Turkmenistan is an observer state in the Organisation of Turkic States, the Türksoy community, and a member of the United Nations.
