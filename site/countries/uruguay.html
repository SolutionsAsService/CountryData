History

Colonial History
Originally colonized by the Spanish, the territory of modern Uruguay was contested between Spanish and Portuguese influences.
Independence and Early Nationhood
Uruguay declared independence in 1825 following conflicts including the Cisplatine War, and was recognized as an independent nation in 1828.
Modern History
Over the decades, Uruguay evolved into a stable democracy known for its progressive policies and social welfare systems.
