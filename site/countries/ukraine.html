Historical Summary

Early History
Settlement dates back to 32,000 BC with various ancient cultures. The area was a key centre of East Slavic culture under the state of Kievan Rus'.
Golden Age of Kyiv
The state of Kievan Rus' reached its zenith during the 10th and 11th centuries with significant cultural and military achievements.
Foreign Domination
The area was contested and ruled by various powers over the centuries, including the Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth, the Ottoman Empire, and the Russian Empire.
Cossack Hetmanate
A Cossack military quasi-state formed in the mid-17th century, experiencing significant conflict and autonomy restrictions over time.
19th and Early 20th Century
The rise of Ukrainian nationalism and significant turmoil during World War I and the subsequent years leading to the establishment of the Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic as part of the Soviet Union.

Recent History

Independence
Gained in 1991 as the Soviet Union dissolved, followed by the adoption of a new constitution in 1996.
Euromaidan and Recent Conflicts
Mass demonstrations led to a new government in 2014. Ongoing conflict with Russia including the annexation of Crimea and war in the Donbas.

Economic Overview

Status
Developing country, the poorest in Europe by nominal GDP per capita, but one of the largest grain exporters globally pre-war.
Corruption
Remains a significant issue.
