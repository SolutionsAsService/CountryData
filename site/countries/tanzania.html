History

Pre-Colonial
The region was home to diverse kingdoms and established trade networks.
Colonial Period
Colonized by Germany (in parts) and later administered by the British as Tanganyika; Zanzibar maintained a distinct history as a sultanate.
Post Independence
Following independence, Tanganyika and Zanzibar merged in 1964 to form Tanzania. The country experienced a long period of single-party rule followed by a transition to multiparty democracy.
